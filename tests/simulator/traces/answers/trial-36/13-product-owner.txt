    verification: |
      Each test is a native MQTT test. It reuses the WiFi-up setup, the way the stub client reports `connected()` as false, and the event capture that the existing MQTT link-drop tests already use. No new helper is added.
      - **Silent reset:** connect, listen for `mqtt/connected` and `mqtt/disconnected`, then call `resetReconnect()`. Expect exactly one `mqtt/disconnected` and `getState()` equal to `Disconnected`. Reconnect and expect the next `mqtt/connected` to come after that `mqtt/disconnected`.
      - **Silent reset, no double count:** call `resetReconnect()` while not connected. Expect no `mqtt/disconnected` event.
      - **Link flap:** drop the link without calling `disconnect()` and reconnect, twice, calling `loop()` after each drop. Expect the new loss counter in `MQTTStatistics` to equal 2, and `connectCount` to be unchanged in meaning. A deliberate `disconnect()` is checked separately: it counts as one loss, once.
      - **Frozen uptime:** connect, let time pass, and drop the link. Call `loop()` and expect `getStatistics().uptime` to be 0 during the outage, and to count again from 0 after the reconnection.
      - The existing MQTT suite stays green.
