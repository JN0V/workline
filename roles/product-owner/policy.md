- Every `close` quotes its evidence, as written: `{path, text}` from a file,
  or `{issue, text}` from an issue's body or comments. The engine finds the
  quote again or does nothing.
- Never close an issue because nobody wants it: refusing a need is a
  person's decision.
- A closing undone by a person is a wrong closing; it puts that kind of
  act back to a person. When in doubt, skip.
