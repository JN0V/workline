# A lens of a spec (#128): a Need and a Scope that do not match.
subject: spec
judge:
  question: >-
    Is this finding about the spec true: does the Need quoted ask for work
    in a part of the project the Scope does not name, or does the Scope
    name what no part of the Need asks? Answer no if the Scope covers it,
    by name or as a folder or a part holding it, or if the finding only
    guesses.
---
**Out of scope.** Compare the Need, and the Verification, with the Scope:
the Scope is what the builder may touch. Work the Need implies in a part
of the project the Scope leaves out — a file, a command, a doc, a test the
Verification needs — is a finding, its cause the words that ask it. A part
the Scope names that nothing in the Need asks is one too, its cause the
Scope's words. A spec with no Scope: a finding quoting its Need.
